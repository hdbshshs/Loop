How Payments Work

When you check out, your order is held while you pay on Stripe's secure page. Your order goes through only after your card is approved. If the payment is declined, blocked or not finished in 30 minutes, the order is cancelled, the items go back on sale, and your card is not charged.

Payment security

Card numbers are typed into Stripe's page, not Loop's. Loop never sees or stores your card number. Stripe checks each payment for fraud and asks your bank to approve it, and tells Loop the result. We also limit how many checkouts and sign-in attempts one person can make, and we log every payment event.
