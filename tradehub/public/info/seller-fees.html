Seller Fees and Payouts

Fees

Loop keeps 9% of items priced under $100 and 13% of items priced at $100 or more. You see the fee as you type your price. There is no listing fee.

How you get paid

You are paid 14 days after the buyer pays, as long as the order has shipped. The money goes to your bank account through Stripe. You set this up once on the Payouts page, which takes you to Stripe to confirm your identity and add your bank details. You cannot list items until that is done.

Good to know

  • Sellers must be in the United States for now.
  • Orders cancelled before they ship are refunded to the buyer and you are not paid for them.
  • Loop sometimes lowers the price a buyer pays using its own money. That never reduces your payout: you are paid your listed price minus the fee.
