Seller Guide

  1. Make your account. Sign up and confirm your email from the link we send.
  2. Set up payouts. Go to Payouts and finish Stripe's setup.
  3. List your item. Add clear photos, an honest title and description, the category, the condition, and a price from $1 to $50,000.
  4. Watch My sales. A paid order shows up with the buyer's name and address and a countdown for how long you have to ship.
  5. Ship it. Mark it shipped with a carrier, a tracking number and an arrival estimate. The buyer's countdown starts from your estimate, so be realistic.
  6. Get paid. 14 days after the buyer paid, once it has shipped.

Need to cancel? You can cancel an order before it ships from My sales. The buyer is refunded in full.
