About Loop

Loop is a marketplace where people sell to people. Sellers list what they have, buyers pay on a secure page, the seller ships it, and a live countdown shows when it should arrive.

How it works

  1. A seller lists an item with photos, a price and a description.
  2. A buyer pays. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and never touch Loop.
  3. The seller sees the order and the buyer's shipping address, ships it, and sets an arrival estimate.
  4. The buyer sees a live countdown to that estimate.
  5. The seller is paid 14 days after the buyer paid, once the order has shipped.
