Shipping and the Delivery Timer

Loop shows a live countdown instead of vague status words.

  • Seller ships within: after you pay, a countdown shows how long the seller has to ship.
  • Arrives in: after the seller marks the order shipped, Loop estimates the delivery date from road distance and current weather alerts when route estimates are configured and both addresses have U.S. ZIP codes. Otherwise, it uses the seller's selected estimate.
  • Delivered: when you tap Mark as received, the timer is replaced with the delivery date.

The route estimate is a rough calculation based on about 500 road miles per transit day, with an extra day for a severe current alert along the route. It is not a carrier guarantee or live carrier data. Use the tracking number on your order for carrier updates. If the timer runs out and your item has not arrived, message the seller first, then contact us.
