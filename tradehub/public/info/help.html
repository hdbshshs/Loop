Help

Where is my order?

Open Your Orders. Each order has a live countdown. See how the timer works.

I did not get my confirmation email

Check your spam folder, then use the Confirm your email link at the top of the site to send it again. Make sure the address is spelled right.

I forgot my password

On the sign in page, tap Forgot your password and we will email you a link that works for one hour.

How do I cancel an order?

You can cancel before the seller ships it, from Your Orders. You are asked for a reason, and you are refunded in full to your card. We send the refund right away, and your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 business days.

My payment did not go through

The order is cancelled and your card is not charged. Check your card details and try again, or use another card.

When do sellers get paid?

14 days after the buyer paid, once the order has shipped. See Seller Fees and Payouts.
