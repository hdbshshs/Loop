Safety and Trust

Tips for buyers

  • Pay only through Loop. Never pay a seller by bank transfer, gift card or another site.
  • Check the seller's profile and how many items they have sold.
  • Use the delivery timer. If it runs out and nothing arrived, message the seller first, then contact us.
  • Only tap Mark as received when the item is in your hands and as described.

Tips for sellers

  • Ship only orders that show up in My sales as paid.
  • Add a real carrier and tracking number, and set an honest arrival estimate.
  • Never ask a buyer to pay or talk outside Loop.

Report a problem

See a listing that breaks our Prohibited Items rules, a scam, or an unsafe product? Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the link to the listing and what is wrong. We remove listings that break the rules and may close accounts.
