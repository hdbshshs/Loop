Refunds and Shipping

Last updated: [DATE]

Shipping

Sellers ship orders directly to the buyer's address and add a carrier and tracking number in Loop. Sellers must ship within [NUMBER] days of a paid order.

Cancelling before it ships

Buyers and sellers can cancel an order that has not shipped. Whoever cancels is asked for a reason. The buyer is refunded in full to the card they paid with. Loop sends the refund right away, and the buyer's bank decides when it shows on the statement, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

After it ships

A seller can still refund the buyer in full, with a reason, at any time until the seller has been paid. Once an item is marked shipped, contact the seller first at the address on your order. If you cannot sort it out, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] within [NUMBER] days of delivery and we will look at it. Do not mark an order as received until the item has arrived and is as described.

Item not received or not as described

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with your order number. We may refund the buyer and recover the money from the seller. Sellers who repeatedly fail to deliver can be removed.

Chargebacks

If a buyer disputes a payment with their bank, we may hold the seller's payout for that order while it is reviewed.
