Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

Username, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash), shipping address, your orders and listings, photos you upload, and basic technical logs (such as IP address, used for security and rate limiting).

Card details

Card numbers are entered on Stripe's secure page and never reach our servers. We only get the result of the payment and a payment reference.

Who sees what

Sellers see the name and shipping address of buyers who order from them, so they can ship. Other users see your username, your listings, and how many items you have sold. We share data with Stripe (payments and seller payouts) and our email provider (account emails), and with authorities when the law requires.

Photos and Snap & Sell

Photos you upload for a listing are stored on our server and, when configured, sent to our image provider (remove.bg) to remove the background. Photos you add with Snap & Sell are also sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to read the item and write the listing. Do not photograph anything private.

How long we keep it
